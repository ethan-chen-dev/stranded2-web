Stranded II game assets
=======================

The files under gfx/, sfx/, skies/, sprites/, maps/ and sys/ on this site are
the original assets of Stranded II (models, textures, sounds, music, maps and
definition files), copyright Peter Schauß / Unreal Software.
https://www.unrealsoftware.de

They are hosted here with the permission of the author (granted by e-mail on
2026-09-06) solely so that this non-commercial browser remake can run. They are
not covered by the CC BY-NC-SA license of the remake's source code and may not
be redistributed or reused outside this site without the author's permission.

Remake source code: https://github.com/ethan-chen-dev/stranded2-web
Original game:      https://www.unrealsoftware.de/game_stranded2.php
